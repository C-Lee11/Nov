Event hosted by Harvest Table:

A Community Thanksgiving Dinner is hosted by Marlboro Helping Neighbors. This Thanksgiving event gives 100% of ticket and donation proceeds to the Harvest Hands Food Bank of Monmouth. If you bring 3 canned goods, you can get $2 off of EACH ticket

Parking is free with an accessable entrance; you just need to find a spot.

Donations can be made. $10 = 20 meals, $25 = 50 meals, $50 = 100 meals.
